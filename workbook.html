The workbook

A problem set for every page in the series, with each page matched to the parts of the standard books that cover the same ground. Read the page, read the book section beside it, then work the problems before opening the answers.

  1. Numeric answers are computed, not typed. Each one runs through the same Black–Scholes engine the pages use, and the repo's numeric checker evaluates every answer, so none of them can drift away from the arithmetic.
  2. Print it. "Print problems" gives you a clean sheet with the answers folded away; "Print with answers" gives you the key.
  3. The reading names topics, not page numbers. Chapter numbers change between editions, so each entry names the chapter or topic to look for. Natenberg's titles are from the second edition.
  4. "Build it" boxes are for coding. They turn a page into a short Python exercise on real or simulated data.

Reading suggestions point at the standard references for each topic: Natenberg for options trading, Sinclair for volatility trading, Hull for derivatives theory, Gatheral for the volatility surface, Bennett for volatility products, Taleb for hedging in practice, Harris for market microstructure, Kirchner for merger arbitrage, plus a few original papers where the idea started. Check chapter titles against your own edition.
