The Forward Is the Real Underlying

Options are not priced off the stock price. Buy the share, borrow the money, collect the dividends, sell it forward — the P&L is exactly zero at every outcome, which is what fixes the price and why a futures curve is carry rather than a forecast. Then contract specs, Black-76, and the finding that inverts page 08 completely: on a futures contract, both American calls and puts get exercised early, because a European futures option can trade below its intrinsic value.
